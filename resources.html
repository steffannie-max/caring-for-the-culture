When you need someone other than yourself

Lines, directories, and offices worth knowing about before you need them.

Use what you need. You don’t have to go through everything.

If you need more than this tool

You know how to reach people. Here are some you may not have on hand.

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
  • BlackLine: 1-800-604-5841 (peer support, by and for Black, Indigenous, and people of color)
  • Trans Lifeline: 877-565-8860
  • StrongHearts Native Helpline: 1-844-762-8483

Therapy directories

Therapists who do not need racism explained to them.

  • Therapy for Black Girls: therapyforblackgirls.com
  • Therapy for Black Men: therapyforblackmen.org
  • Latinx Therapy: latinxtherapy.com
  • Asian Mental Health Collective: asianmhc.org
  • National Queer & Trans Therapists of Color Network: nqttcn.com
  • WeRNative: wernative.org
  • Inclusive Therapists: inclusivetherapists.com

Professional and workplace support

For when the workplace itself is the issue.

  • ABNAO: Alliance of Black Nurses Association of Oregon
  • NBNA: National Black Nurses Association
  • NAHN: National Association of Hispanic Nurses
  • AAPINA: Asian American Pacific Islander Nurses Association
  • NANAINA: National Alaska Native American Indian Nurses Association
  • Your union (if you have one)
  • Your EAP (Employee Assistance Program)
  • Trauma Informed Oregon: traumainformedoregon.org

Community voices

Listen to people who get it. Find your people.

  • Black Care Matters Podcast: blackcarematters.org — created by ABNAO, centering Black nurses in Oregon. Twelve episodes of honest conversation about racism in healthcare and the work of staying whole within it.
  • NBNA Podcasts: nbna.org/resources/podcasts — The NBNA Podcast and The Health Disparities Podcast, covering broader nursing leadership and patient health equity.

If you may need to escalate

Know what exists before you need it. This is not legal advice.

  • EEOC: Equal Employment Opportunity Commission. Federal complaints related to workplace discrimination. eeoc.gov
  • Oregon BOLI: Bureau of Labor and Industries. State-level complaints. oregon.gov/boli
  • Your employer's ombuds office (if there is one) for confidential consultation
  • Legal Aid Services of Oregon: lasoregon.org for free legal consultation if you qualify

If You Need More Support

Use what fits. You do not have to go through everything.

  • Talk to someone you trust
  • Use workplace supports if they feel safe
  • Seek clinical support if needed
